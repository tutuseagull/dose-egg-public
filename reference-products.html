Reference Products

Apps and tools to learn from — not competitors, but adjacent practices worth understanding before designing.

PD — Direct reference

Apps built specifically for Parkinson's patients.

StrivePD
PD-specific tracking app. The closest direct competitor. Worth understanding what they built and where the gaps are.
PD
Health tracking — tone + IA reference

DoseEgg's sweet spot sits between Bearable (depth) and Apple Journal / Finch (companion tone). Explicitly not Oura / Apple Fitness performance framing.

Bearable
Symptom, medication, and lifestyle tracking. Closest to DoseEgg's data model and intent. Study the log types and how they surface patterns.
Data model
Apple Health
Unified timeline, system-level. Reference for how to present diverse data types in a single view without overwhelming the user.
Timeline
Apple Journal
Reflective, calm, low-pressure. The tone DoseEgg wants to borrow — logging as adding to your day, not checking a box.
Tone
Finch
Wellbeing companion with a gentle character. Companion tone without performance pressure. Useful for understanding how to make a care-oriented app feel warm, not clinical.
Tone
Oura
Sleep and readiness tracking with a daily score. Well-executed — but the score model is what DoseEgg is moving away from. Study it to understand what to avoid.
Anti-pattern ref
Type 1 Diabetes — structural analog

T1D is the closest design precedent: same logic as PD (lacks X, supplement X, monitor), more mature ecosystem. The whole category is worth studying.

Dexcom G7
Gold standard CGM (continuous glucose monitor). Study how they surface "time in range" as the headline metric — this is the T1D equivalent of DoseEgg's potential "time in ON" metric.
CGM
Abbott LibreLink
More accessible CGM, strong in Europe and Asia. Good for seeing an alternative UX approach to the same underlying data problem.
CGM
Omnipod 5
Automated insulin delivery — adjusts dose in real time based on CGM readings. Dream future analog for PD (automated levodopa). Study the closed-loop UX: how do you design for a system that acts without asking?
Closed-loop
Tandem Control-IQ
Another leading automated insulin delivery system. Compare with Omnipod 5 to see two approaches to the same UX problem.
Closed-loop
Nightscout
Open source, patient-built CGM monitoring. The T1D community built this because official tools weren't good enough — a huge cultural parallel to what DoseEgg could become for YOPD.
Community-built
Tidepool
Aggregates data from all CGM and pump devices into one place. Relevant to DoseEgg's unified timeline concept — how do you bring multiple data streams together cleanly?
Aggregator
Key metric to borrow: T1D uses "time in range" — % of hours glucose stays in the target zone — as the single headline outcome. DoseEgg probably needs an equivalent: time in ON or functional hours per day. That one number is what a patient checks each morning and what a doctor scans in 10 seconds. Worth defining before designing the Trend/Analyze view.
