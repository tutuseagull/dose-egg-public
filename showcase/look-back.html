Stretches

Runs of days where the gaps moved away from the weeks around them.

By 3 months

How far from plan the gaps landed, typically.

A stretch is at least 7 days in a row where the gaps sat 20 minutes or more away from the four weeks either side. Shorter or smaller changes are left alone, because on any record some weeks are a little different by chance.

Only doses you confirmed count. A dose filled in from your schedule is a plan, not an observation, so it is left out. Days with no entries are gaps in the chart, never zero.

The app finds when. Only you know why, so naming a stretch is yours.
