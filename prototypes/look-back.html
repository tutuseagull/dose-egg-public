Count days with no entries

A day with no entries is a day this app knows nothing about. It is not a day with nothing in it, and it is never counted as a zero.

Leaving those days in the count keeps every sentence on this page honest about what it could not see. Taking them out answers a different question: what do the days you did log look like on their own. Both are real questions. Neither is the correct one.
