Today at four widths

All four frames load the same file, so anything changed in hifi-daily-loop.html shows up in all of them. Each frame's own tune panel works independently — handy for A/B-ing one setting against a fixed reference.

The corner that matters is 375 at 125% — narrowest phone, largest text.
What to look for. Text width after padding is width − gutter×2 − rail-indent − card-pad×2 − 24 (that last 24 keeps the chevron clear). At the defaults that's 235pt on a 375 phone and 280pt on the Air. Push text to 125% without changing padding and the 375 column is where wrapping, collisions and truncation appear first — if it holds there, the rest will.
